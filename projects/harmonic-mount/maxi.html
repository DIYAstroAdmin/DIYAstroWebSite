Maxi variant

A heavy-duty observatory mount for large refractors, 10–12″ reflectors and dual-scope rigs, intended for permanent pier installation.

Overview

Ideal for: Observatory piers, larger OTAs, dual imaging rigs and long focal-length work.

Design in progress. Figures on this page are design targets and will be updated with measured values as prototypes are built and tested.

Specifications

Build notes

  • The Maxi uses 24 V and TMC5160 drivers for the extra torque needed at higher slew speeds; the controller PCB has a jumper-selectable driver footprint.
  • Use crossed-roller bearings on both axes — the moment loads from a 25 kg payload are too high for angular-contact pairs in this size.
  • Designed for permanent pier installation; a polar-alignment adjuster with fine-pitch screws is included.

Mini variant

A compact, travel-friendly mount for small refractors, camera lenses and guide-scope rigs. Light enough to fly with, rigid enough for long exposures.

Overview

Ideal for: Small refractors (60–80 mm), DSLR / mirrorless camera lenses, travel and back-garden quick setups.

Design in progress. Figures on this page are design targets and will be updated with measured values as prototypes are built and tested.

Specifications

Belt reduction2.5:1 (GT2 belt)

Build notes

  • The Mini is the best starting point if you have never built a mount: it uses the fewest machined parts and most of the housing can be 3D-printed for a first prototype.
  • Keep the belt tension modest — size 14 input shafts are small, and over-tensioning will load the wave generator bearing.
  • Without a counterweight, balance in Dec is still worth doing: it reduces motor current and improves guiding.

Midi variant

The all-rounder. Carries a typical 80–130 mm refractor or 8″ reflector with a full imaging train, guiding and accessories.

Overview

Ideal for: Mid-sized refractors, 6–8″ Newtonians and RCs, permanent or semi-permanent back-garden setups.

Design in progress. Figures on this page are design targets and will be updated with measured values as prototypes are built and tested.

Specifications

Primary reductionSize 17 strain-wave gear, 100:1
Belt reduction3:1 (GT2 belt)
Total ratio300:1
MotorsNEMA 17, 0.9° stepper
Stepper driversTMC2209 (UART), 256 microsteps
Resolution (per microstep)0.042″
Target payload12 kg
Mount head weight≈ 4.5 kg
Maximum slew rate3°/s
SaddleDual Vixen / Losmandy
BaseHeavy-duty tripod or pier adapter
CounterweightOptional (recommended above 7 kg)
Power12 V DC, 3 A
Approximate parts cost≈ £700

A full bill of materials with part numbers and suppliers will be included in the download pack.

Build notes

  • The Midi shares its controller and firmware with the Mini; only the configuration file (gear ratios and motor current) changes.
  • Housings are designed to be machined from standard 6082 aluminium bar with a manual mill and lathe; a CNC version of the drawings will follow.
  • The counterweight shaft is threaded M12 and can be omitted for lighter payloads.
